## 🔍 About the Project

Digital payments have transformed the way India transacts. With the rapid adoption of UPI, understanding digital payment security has become increasingly important.

**UPI Security Lab** is an educational and research-driven web experience designed to make complex cybersecurity concepts easier to understand through interactive visualizations, simulated fraud scenarios and structured research.

Rather than presenting cybersecurity as a collection of technical definitions, the platform encourages visitors to explore, interact, investigate and learn.

Developed in the academic context of **VSIT Student Research Symposium 2026**.

## 🚨 Threats Under Investigation

The platform explores several common digital payment fraud techniques:

- **Collect Request Fraud:** Deceptive payment requests designed to trick users into authorizing transactions.
- **Fake QR Code Scams:** Manipulated QR codes used to mislead users into making payments.
- **Fake Customer Care Scams:** Impersonation of customer support representatives to obtain sensitive information.
- **Screen-Sharing Scams:** Misuse of remote-access applications to exploit user trust and device permissions.
- **SIM Swap Fraud:** Unauthorized control of a victim's mobile number to compromise account security.

Each threat is explored through its attack process, warning signs, potential consequences and prevention strategies.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand the architecture and functioning of UPI transactions.
- Identify common digital payment fraud techniques.
- Investigate social-engineering methods used by cybercriminals.
- Explore existing UPI security and authentication mechanisms.
- Improve awareness of safe digital payment practices.
- Present cybersecurity research through interactive visual storytelling.

- Strong typography and visual hierarchy.

- Interactive diagrams and meaningful data visualizations.
- Purposeful scroll-triggered animations.

## 🔐 Security Awareness

This project is strictly educational.

- No real banking transactions are performed.
- No actual UPI credentials are required.
- Never enter real UPI PINs, OTPs or banking credentials into simulations.
- Example scam scenarios are intended for awareness and learning.

**Remember:** A UPI PIN is used to authorize certain transactions, not to receive money.
